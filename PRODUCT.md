## Platform

## Stack

Astro, static output, deployed to GitHub Pages at the custom domain zeroth.me. Content must be editable later through Pages CMS (pagescms.org), so all profile content lives in Git-tracked data files (YAML/Markdown) with a `.pages.yml` config, never hard-coded in components.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Founders and teams who want an idea taken to a production, AI-native product (Zeroth consultancy clients).
- Research groups and scientists who need imaging / ML research software and computational methods (collaborators).

Both arrive from a LinkedIn link, an email signature, or a referral, and want to know within seconds who Abhishek is, whether he can do their kind of work, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

zeroth.me is the personal profile of Abhishek Patil: scientist (PhD, biomedical imaging; Research Fellow, Monash University) and software architect (15+ years; founder of Zeroth, an independent consultancy). A single page, no blog for now. Success = a qualified visitor emails abhishek@zeroth.me.

One person who is both a working imaging/ML scientist and a production systems architect: firmware deployed on 3,000+ monitoring units, a government digitisation programme, an EHR platform, identity work at Auth0/Okta, and microscopy pipelines adopted by a university imaging facility.
